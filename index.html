About me

I’m a 3D artist trained at Howest Digital Arts and Entertainment (DAE) in Kortrijk, Belgium. I like working on both ends of a scene: building procedural tools in Houdini that lay out whole landscapes, and then zooming in on a single prop until every scratch and rivet tells a story. Everything comes together in Unreal Engine, where I spend most of my time on lighting and mood.
